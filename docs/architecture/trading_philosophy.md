* **SET 1 (Long-Term Investment Horizon):** HTF = 1M  | MTF = 1W | LTF = 1D
* **SET 2 (Position Trading Horizon):** HTF = 1W  | MTF = 1D | LTF = 4H
* **SET 3 (Swing Trading Horizon):** HTF = 1D  | MTF = 4H | LTF = 1H
* **SET 4 (Intraday Scalping Horizon):** HTF = 4H  | MTF = 1H | LTF = 15M

## 2. THE THREE TIMEFRAME RESPONSIBILITIES

### A. Higher Timeframe (HTF) Engine: Reality Selection
* **Objective:** Establish institutional bias and premium/discount range boundaries.
* **Bullish Requirement:** Price must reside above structural demand matrix limits and display consecutive higher-high body closes.
* **Bearish Requirement:** Price must reside beneath structural supply matrix limits and display consecutive lower-low body closes.

### B. Medium Timeframe (MTF) Engine: Opportunity Capture
* **Objective:** Wait for order flow correction to confirm structural realignment.
* **Process:** Price must pull back into an unmitigated HTF institutional array (Order Block or Fair Value Gap imbalance). Once mitigation is confirmed, the engine watches for an internal change of character (CHOCH) or body break matching the primary HTF bias vector.

### C. Lower Timeframe (LTF) Engine: Precision Execution Trigger
* **Objective:** Minimize stop-loss distance and hunt localized traps.
* **Process:** Once MTF is aligned, the LTF engine scans for a predatory liquidity sweep of internal range extremes (Equal Highs / Equal Lows). Following the sweep, it requires a displacement leg leading to a micro Break of Structure (BOS).

## 3. CAPITAL INTERCEPTION & FIREWALL GATES

### A. Trade Management Parameterization
* **Entry Placement:** Positioned at the micro BOS displacement origin or the unmitigated LTF mitigation level.
* **Stop-Loss Protection:** Locked strictly past the absolute invalidation point of the LTF liquidity sweep wick.
* **Take-Profit Extraction:** Anchored at the unmitigated external range liquidity boundary of the HTF structural target.
* **The Golden Ratio:** If the calculated reward distance divided by the risk distance is `< 4.0`, the signal is dropped immediately.

### B. Risk Control
* Maximum position risk allocation is capped at exactly `1%` of total portfolio capital. Sizing calculations use the stop-loss distance to dynamically adjust transaction volume.

### C. News Filtering Gate
* High-impact macro data windows (`CPI`, `FOMC`, etc.) activate a local execution lock 30 minutes prior to the release window and remain locked until 30 minutes post-release.

### D. Portfolio Exposure Isolation
* The Portfolio Engine views correlated assets (e.g., `BTC`, `ETH`, `SOL`) as a unified structural block. If total correlation risk exposure reaches `3%`, all additional signals are intercepted and held in queue mode.
